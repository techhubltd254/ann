--- 1.3 KEY MARKETING PILLARS & WHAT TO PHOTOGRAPH ---

PILLAR 1: BLUE ECONOMY & FISHERIES (Flagship)
  - Fish landing sites and modern fish ponds (KEMFSED project)
  - Community fish farming (tilapia ponds in Tezo, Mnarani)
  - Women in fisheries (14,759 women supported)
  - Mariculture / seaweed farming along coastline
  - Mangrove conservation and restoration
  - Boat building and traditional dhows
  - Deep-sea fishing vessels

PILLAR 2: AGRICULTURE & AGRO-PROCESSING
  - Milly Fruit Processing Factory (Sh343M, Sabaki) — fruit processing
  - Mango, coconut, cashew nut, and baobab value chains
  - Digitally Enabled Agripreneurship Model (100,000+ farmers)
  - Livestock farming and dairy
  - Cashew nut and coconut plantations (government planting program)
  - Irrigation schemes and water harvesting structures

PILLAR 3: TOURISM & HOSPITALITY
  - Watamu Marine National Park — coral reefs, snorkeling, turtles
  - Malindi Marine Park — water sports, marine life
  - Gede Ruins — ancient Swahili city,历史文化
  - Arabuko Sokoke Forest — birdwatching, unique species
  - Vipingo Ridge — PGA golf course, MICE tourism, luxury resort
  - Kilifi Creek / Mnarani ruins — cliff-top views
  - Mida Creek — boardwalk, birdwatching, sunset
  - Beach resorts and boutique hotels along the coast

PILLAR 4: INDUSTRY & INVESTMENT (The Kilifi Boom)
  - EPZ factories (24 enterprises, Sh9B investment, 14,000 jobs)
  - Vipingo Special Economic Zone (flagship SEZ project)
  - Mariakani Industrial Park (proposed)
  - Fruit processing, textile, and manufacturing plants
  - Mombasa-Nairobi SGR connection benefits
  - Export processing and value addition

PILLAR 5: ENERGY & INFRASTRUCTURE
  - Kipevu Oil Terminal (Mombasa connection benefits Kilifi)
  - Wind and solar energy projects
  - Road network development (Kilifi-Malindi highway)
  - My Kilifi App (digital government services)
  - Water supply projects and dams

PILLAR 6: CULTURE, SPORTS & YOUTH
  - Traditional Swahili festivals and cultural events
  - Sports infrastructure (stadiums, football academies)
  - Youth polytechnics and vocational training
  - Golf tourism (Vipingo Ridge PGA Baobab Course)
  - Youth empowerment programmes

PILLAR 7: ENVIRONMENT & CLIMATE ACTION
  - Mangrove restoration projects
  - Climate change adaptation programmes
  - Natural resource management
  - Forest conservation (Arabuko Sokoke)

PILLAR 8: EDUCATION & ICT

C. Manufacturing & Industry — EPZs, SEZs, value addition

--- 2.3 KEY MARKETING PILLARS & WHAT TO PHOTOGRAPH ---

PILLAR 1: PORT & MARITIME (Flagship — East Africa's Largest Port)
  - Port of Mombasa (Kilindini Harbour) — container ships, cranes
  - Second Container Terminal (Japanese-funded, 1.2M TEU capacity)
  - Mombasa Port Area Road Development (Dongo Kundu Bypass)
  - Kipevu Oil Terminal
  - Mombasa Special Economic Zone (Dongo Kundu)
  - Fishing industry — Liwatoni Fisheries Complex
  - Deep-sea fishing exhibition and commercial fishing
  - Shimoni Fish Port development
  - Cruise ship terminal — Viking Yidun first Asian cruise ship (2026)

PILLAR 2: TOURISM & MICE (Flagship)
  - Fort Jesus (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
  - Mombasa Old Town — narrow streets, Swahili architecture, balconies
  - Mombasa Tusks (Mackinnon Market area)
  - Mombasa International Convention Centre (proposed, 5,000 capacity)
  - Nyali Beach, Bamburi Beach, Shanzu Beach — resorts, water sports
  - Haller Park — nature trail, giraffes, crocodiles
  - Mamba Village — crocodile farm
  - Mombasa Marine National Park — snorkeling, coral reefs
  - Nguuni Nature Sanctuary
  - Mombasa Carnival and cultural festivals
  - Spice Market and Maasai Market
  - Mombasa Cruise Tourism (growing, 21,867 projected by 2035)

PILLAR 3: BLUE ECONOMY & FISHERIES
  - Fish landing sites and cold storage (KEMFSED project)
  - Seaweed farming (emerging, women-led)
  - Aquaculture and fish farming
  - Boat building industry
  - Mangrove conservation and eco-tourism
  - Marine biotechnology potential

PILLAR 4: MANUFACTURING & EPZ
  - 28 EPZ enterprises in Mombasa County (highest in coastal region)
  - Mombasa SEZ (Japanese-funded master plan)
  - Textile and apparel manufacturing
  - Agro-processing (Mombasa as gateway for exports)
  - Dongo Kundu Special Economic Zone
  - Industrial fisheries and fish processing

PILLAR 5: TRANSPORT & LOGISTICS
  - Mombasa-Nairobi Standard Gauge Railway (SGR)
  - Moi International Airport
  - Likoni Ferry crossing
  - Nyali Bridge, Makupa Causeway
  - Mombasa Southern Bypass (Dongo Kundu)
  - Northern Corridor transit route (to Uganda, Rwanda, DRC, South Sudan)
  - Logistics warehouses and freight forwarding

PILLAR 6: TRADE & COMMERCE
  - Mombasa CBD — business district, banks, corporate offices
  - Kongowea Market (largest open-air market in coastal Kenya)
  - Mackinnon Market — spices, fabrics, crafts
  - Single Business Permit automation (30% revenue increase)
  - Business registration (15-minute processing)
  - Cross-border trade (gateway to 500M+ East African consumers)

PILLAR 7: SMART CITY & DIGITAL TRANSFORMATION
  - Smart City Flagship Project (under Digital Transformation)
  - E-services platform automation
  - Digital parking and revenue collection
  - Online Single Business Permit system

PILLAR 8: CULTURE & HERITAGE
  - Swahili culture and traditions
  - Fort Jesus Museum and historical exhibits
  - Old Town heritage walk
  - Mombasa Carnival
  - Swahili cuisine (biryani, grilled fish, street food)
  - Traditional dhow sailing
  - Portuguese, Arab, British colonial architecture

PILLAR 9: EDUCATION & SPORTS
  - Technical University of Mombasa
  - ECDE programmes (98 public centres)
  - Elimu Fund (capitation grant for learners)
  - Vocational training centres
  - Sports infrastructure and youth talent programmes
  - Mombasa Women in Sports initiatives

PILLAR 10: URBAN DEVELOPMENT & HOUSING
  - Affordable housing projects
  - Urban renewal and planning
  - Shanzu, Changamwe, Old Town development zones
  - Serikali Mtaani (decentralized service delivery)
  - Waste management and sanitation
