About Murang'a

Murang'a County is the heart of Kenya's tea country. Nestled in the central highlands, its rolling hills produce some of the world's finest single-origin black tea. The county is also home to spectacular waterfalls, trout-filled rivers, and the famous Sagana rafting experience.
