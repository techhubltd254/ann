KENYA NATIONAL EXHIBITION, IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCE & COMMERCE PLATFORM
System Design & Architecture Blueprint 


Prepared for KICC, Nairobi — July 2026



1. Executive Summary
Kenya hosts exhibitions, conventions, and trade showcases across KICC, Bomas of Kenya, and county-level venues. Still, these remain fragmented, physical, one-off events with no persistent digital layer, no unified discovery, no data-driven matchmaking between exhibitors and buyers, and no mechanism to convert a visitor’s interest into a completed transaction.
This document specifies a single national platform combining four functions: (1) an exhibition and convention management system open to any verified individual, SME, school, or government entity; (2) a tiered immersive digital-twin experience layer letting anyone experience any county across every economic sector; (3) a commerce layer with escrow and courier fulfilment; and (4) an AI intelligence layer that personalises discovery, grades vendors, and monetises visibility.
This document incorporates all 13 competency areas from the KICC specification — including the specific technology stack (Laravel/PHP, Python, JavaScript, Bootstrap, MySQL/MariaDB), hands-on server administration, database administration, SDLC process, QA/testing, software maintenance, and technical documentation — alongside the platform’s existing architecture, security, and content design.
All examples of specific attractions are illustrative. Every county has its own unique attractions. The 3-tier immersive model (360°, cinematic 4D, interactive twin) applies uniformly across all 47 counties and 7 economic sectors.
2. Global Benchmark Analysis
Virtual Singapore proves the digital-twin layer at a national scale. Museum of the Future in Dubai proves full sensory immersion design. Irembo Rwanda proves the commission-funded, government-partnered model. Zhejiang rural tourism digital twin proves game-engine viability below national-flagship level. None combine all four layers — that gap is the opportunity.

4. System Architecture — Six Layers
The system is designed as six layers, each independently buildable and independently monetisable, sharing one identity system and one data backbone.
5. Layer 1: Identity & Access
Account types: Individual/Tourist (light + verified), SME/Exhibitor, School/Institution, County Government, National Ministry, Platform Admin, NIS Security. Registration: zero-friction browsing (no account), light registration (phone+OTP), enhanced verification (ID+Huduma), business verification (KRA PIN+MOU). MFA mandatory for all financial and admin accounts. OAuth2/OIDC protocol throughout.
6. Layer 2: Exhibition & Booking Engine
Core objects: Venues → Events → Booths → Tickets → Livestream Channels. Three subscription tiers (Basic KES 500/mo, Premium KES 2,500/mo, Enterprise KES 10,000/mo). Multi-camera livestream (keycam, stagecam, floorcam, boothcam, aerial). Monetisation: free (ad-supported), premium single-event, all-access annual.
7. Layer 3: Immersive Content & Digital Twin
Three tiers: Tier C (interactive twin, Unity/Unreal + Cesium, mobile + touch tables), Tier B (360° spherical, 8K, mobile + web), Tier A (cinematic 4D, 4K projection + motion + scent + wind + temperature, physical booths only). Centralized production team moves county-to-county with drone, 360° camera, cinema camera, LIDAR. Staged rollout: pilot counties first, all 47 counties at Tier B baseline within 18 months.
8. Layer 4: Commerce — Escrow & Courier
Bank-partnered trust account (licensed Kenyan bank). Payment rails: M-Pesa, Airtel Money, T-Kash, Visa/Mastercard, bank EFT, crypto. Eight-step escrow flow. Two-tier dispute resolution (automated < KES 5,000, human-reviewed above). Single exclusive courier partner with nationwide coverage and real-time tracking API.
9. Layer 5: Discovery & Cross-Lingual Search
Unified search service using multilingual vector embeddings (Swahili, English, Sheng → same concept space). Voice search via on-device Whisper ASR at launch. Image search via computer vision pipeline. Graceful degradation to BM25 keyword search on poor connections. All queries route through a single semantic gateway.
10. Layer 6: Intelligence — AI Engine
Hybrid recommender: behavioral (50%) + Trust Score (30%) + demographic (20%) signals. Two-score vendor grading: Trust Score (0-100, earned, publicly visible as A/B/C/D) + Visibility Score (purchasable, labeled Sponsored). Fairness rule: paid placement cannot override earned trust. Predictive analytics (foot traffic, tourism trends, demand sensing). Anomaly detection (fraud, account takeover, review manipulation, infrastructure). Four advertising products.

12. Physical Experience Design
Locations: KICC (flagship, 500 concurrent), Bomas (flagship, 300), JKIA (arrival kiosks), 5-10 county hubs (mini 4D pods, 50-100 each). Each zone has its own sensory design language matching that county’s unique character. Hardware per flagship zone: 2-3 Barco 40K laser projectors, curved 270° or dome screen, D-BOX 6-DOF motion platform, Olorama scent system, L-Acoustics L-ISA spatial audio, ETC lighting, MDG haze machine, Ideum 65" multi-touch table.
13. Mobile Experience Design
Native Unity/Unreal mobile app. Features: Explore Kenya (all counties/sectors), Live Conventions (multi-camera), Marketplace, My Journey, AR Mode (geofence/QR triggered), Offline Tours, Voice Search, Personalized Recommendations. 4D effects (motion, scent, temperature) are exclusive to physical booths; visual/audio parity is maintained through phone gyroscope, binaural audio, and adaptive streaming (144p to 4K).

15. Security & Compliance
15.1 Compliance Frameworks: ISO 27001 (ISMS, pre-launch), PCI DSS v4.0 (from first payment code), Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 (pre-launch registration with the Data Commissioner), SOC 2 Type II (within 12 months).
15.2 Encryption: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit, E2E for sensitive fields, HSM for escrow signing keys.
15.3 Application Security (input validation, OWASP Top 10): All user input is validated and sanitised at the controller level in Laravel (FormRequest validation, prepared statements for SQL queries). XSS protection via Blade escaping + Content-Security-Policy headers. CSRF protection via Laravel’s built-in CSRF token middleware. SQL injection is prevented through Eloquent ORM parameter binding. Rate limiting via Laravel throttle middleware on all API routes. Payload size limits are enforced on file uploads.
15.4 Zero-trust network segmentation: Public Zone (CDN) → Restricted Zone (API/App) → Sensitive Zone (DB/Escrow) → Admin Zone (UI/Ministry). Lateral movement requires re-authentication.
15.5 NIS coordination: Threat intelligence sharing, joint incident response drills (quarterly), KE-CIRT integration.
15.6 Penetration testing: Full-scope pre-launch + quarterly periodic + per-change triggered + continuous bug bounty + annual red team.
16. Government Systems Integration
Integrations structured as live API agreements (not one-time data dumps): eKRA/iTax (business verification), Huduma/eCitizen (identity), NTSA (transport licensing), KWS (parks data), Ministry of Education (schools), Ministry of Tourism (star ratings), Ministry of Agriculture (crop/market data), Ministry of Trade (registrations), Ministry of Health (facilities), National Museums of Kenya (heritage), County Governments (local content). SMS/email/notification services: integrated via Africa’s Talking API (SMS), Laravel Mail + SendGrid/Mailgun (email), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications) for real-time alerts on bookings, escrow status, delivery updates, and system notices.

17. Revenue Model
Three equal revenue streams: escrow commission (2-5% per transaction), exhibitor subscriptions (Basic/Premium/Enterprise), advertising (featured placement, county co-marketing, referral/click-out fees, sponsored livestream banners). Irembo-inspired: the the platform earns when transactions complete, aligning incentives with users, vendors, and government.

19.2 Error Tracking & Monitoring
Sentry is integrated into all application layers (Laravel backend, React frontend, Unity mobile app). Errors tagged by severity (fatal, error, warning, info). Alerts are routed to Slack channels per team. Monthly error review meeting to identify patterns and prioritise fixes.

20. Software Maintenance & Continuous Improvement
Post-launch maintenance is structured into four categories following the ISO/IEC 14764 maintenance framework:
Maintenance workflow: All maintenance tasks are tracked in Linear/Jira. Corrective tasks triaged immediately. Preventive tasks scheduled on a calendar-based cadence. Perfective tasks prioritised in sprint planning alongside feature work. Every maintenance task follows the same SDLC process (Section 18) — no direct-to-production changes except emergency security hotfixes with post-hoc review.
Version upgrade policy: Laravel version upgrades follow the official support timeline (currently Laravel 11 receives security fixes through 2026). PHP version upgrades aligned with active support (PHP 8.2+). MySQL upgrades are performed in staging first, tested against the full regression suite, then production with a rollback plan. Node.js, Redis, Elasticsearch, and other dependencies are similarly tracked through Dependabot alerts.
21. Technical Documentation
Comprehensive documentation is produced and maintained alongside the codebase, not retroactively:
Documentation is stored in a central, version-controlled wiki (GitHub Wiki + /docs directory in the main repository). API documentation is additionally published via a public developer portal (Swagger UI / Stoplight). All documentation is reviewed for accuracy at least quarterly.

22. Risk Analysis

Closing Note
This  document is structured so that each competency area from the KICC specification can be independently evaluated. Every required technology, process, and practice has been explicitly named and described — not assumed or implied.
The core discipline across all layers remains: separate what is proven elsewhere from what is genuinely novel, separate the flagship physical showpiece from the mass-market mobile experience, and never let paid visibility override earned trust. Each county tells its own unique story through its own distinctive attractions, sectors, and cultural heritage.
These three disciplines — honest innovation boundaries, tiered physical-to-mobile experience, and trust-before-profit ranking — are what will let this scale from one convention at KICC to a system serving every county in Kenya.
